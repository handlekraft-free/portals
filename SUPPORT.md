# Support

There are two ways to get help with Open Portals: **community support** (free, best-effort) and **commercial support** (paid, with response-time commitments) from the maintainers.

## Community support (free)

Best-effort, provided by the maintainers and other users. No guaranteed response time.

- **Bug reports and feature requests** → [open a GitHub issue](../../issues/new/choose).
- **Questions and discussion** → use [GitHub Discussions](../../discussions) (or open an issue tagged `question` if Discussions isn't enabled on your fork).
- **Security vulnerabilities** → please **do not** open a public issue. Email **robert@handlekraft.ai** with the details and we'll acknowledge within 2 business days. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for the full policy.

When you file an issue, include:

- Open Portals version (visible in the login footer or via `GET /api/public/version`)
- What you expected vs. what happened
- Steps to reproduce
- Node version, OS, browser
- Relevant server logs (with secrets redacted)

## Commercial support (paid)

The maintainers of Open Portals (handləkraft.ai) offer paid support and services for organizations running this software in production:

- **Installation & deployment** — provisioning, environment setup, schema migration, custom domain + TLS, hardening review.
- **Rebranding & customization** — turn the OSS template into your organization's portal: branding, theming, removing or rewording the gamification layer, custom portals.
- **Integration work** — Google Workspace, SSO/SAML, Stripe, Slack, custom APIs, data imports from existing systems.
- **Priority bug fixes & feature work** — paying customers' issues jump the queue and ship in scheduled releases.
- **Long-term maintenance & upgrades** — staying current with upstream releases, schema migrations, dependency updates, security patches.
- **Training & onboarding** — admin training, end-user documentation, video walkthroughs.
- **SLAs** — defined response-time and uptime commitments for hosted/managed deployments.

Contact: **robert@handlekraft.ai**

In your first email, briefly tell us:

- Your organization and what you do
- Which portals you'd use (Employee / Client / Student / Board)
- Approximate user count
- Whether you want self-hosted or managed hosting
- Timeline

We'll reply within 2 business days with next steps.

## What community support does *not* cover

The community channels are for the OSS project itself. The following are commercial services and are **not** handled in GitHub issues:

- Help installing or operating the software in your specific environment
- Custom feature development for your organization
- One-on-one consultation, code review, or architecture advice
- Production incident response or hands-on debugging of your deployment
- Migrating data from another system
- White-label rebranding or design work

If your question falls into any of those categories, please email **robert@handlekraft.ai**.
